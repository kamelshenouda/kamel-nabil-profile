True size

Standing in a room at 100%, everything is drawn at the size it would look from where you sit. But a screen is a narrow slice of what your eyes take in, so the floor near your feet and the side walls are out of frame and rooms can read shorter than they are: tilt down, turn with A and D, or zoom out to take in the room, and check the distance readout top right. Keys & mousearrows · A D · ⌃W ⌃S
Keyboard: arrow keys move forward, back, left and right · A D look left and right · ⌃W ⌃S zoom in and out (hold to keep zooming) · ⌃D doors open, closed, none · ⌃A show or hide these controls (⌃ is the Control key). Drag to tilt up or down.
Zoom: the + and − buttons on the model, the mouse wheel, pinch, or ⌃W / ⌃S. (On Windows, Ctrl+W closes the tab, so use + or the wheel there.) 1:1 goes back to true size; ⤢ shows the whole apartment. At the door · 1:1 (or H) always takes you back to standing at the main door at true size.
Controls: drag the edge of this panel to resize it (double-click the edge to reset); ‹ hides it.
Overview: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan.
Dimensions: room widths and lengths in metres, inside the walls: on the room labels from above, on the floor lines when standing.
Top right: standing, the zoom (100% is true size: things look their real size and distance; below 100% you see more of the room, but it looks bigger than it is); from above, how far away you are. Then the compass direction you face; the red needle points north.
VR headset: Enter VR puts you in the room at true scale; your own head height sets the eye level. Point a controller at the floor and pull the trigger to move there; push a thumbstick left or right to turn, forward or back to walk.
What is it? Click or tap any piece for its name, real size and finish, outlined in the model; click a floor for the room's size. Esc or × closes it.
Plan: standing, the small plan top right shows where you are and which way you face; click it to walk there.
Tour: Play the tour glides through every view of this layout and stops on any click or key.
Standing: drag to look around; zooming out widens the view. Double-click (or double-tap) the floor to walk there. Walking with the arrows, walls, glass and closed doors stop you and you slide along them.
Walls: frosted glass shows the rooms beyond as soft, light-filled blur; Solid shows painted walls as they will be built. Look: Finishes shows your materials; Model shows a clean white architectural model with the oak kept.
Quality: while you move, the view draws fast; the moment you stop it redraws at full quality with soft contact shading.
