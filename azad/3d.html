Keyboard: arrow keys move forward, back, left and right · W S zoom in and out · A D look left and right · double-tap W or S and hold to tilt up or down.
Zoom: the + and − buttons on the model, the mouse wheel, pinch, or W / S. 1:1 goes back to true size; ⤢ shows the whole apartment again.
Overview: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan.
Dimensions: room widths and lengths in metres, inside the walls: on the room labels from above, on the floor lines when standing.
Top right: the zoom and the compass direction you face. Standing, 100% is true to life: things look their real size and distance. Below 100% you see more of the room, but it looks bigger than it is; the red needle points north.
Standing: drag to look around; zooming out widens the view. Double-click (or double-tap) the floor to walk there.
