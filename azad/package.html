02 · Lighting

Ambient where you live, strong where you work

Warm 2700 K throughout, all dimmable. The glow comes from the edges (coves, washers, shelves) so the ceilings stay clean; bright downlights only over worktops, sinks and baths.

03 · Power, data, security

Routers out of sight, cameras where they count

The fibre box, router and network switch live in a vented bay of the lobby cabinet, beside the electricity board. Wired, slim white access points on the ceilings give full coverage, including the garden. Only the special sockets are drawn; standard sockets follow the usual layout.

Key

Wi-Fi access point (ceiling) · dashed = outdoor Camera: red outdoor, blue indoor Data point (wired) Special socket

Security

Video intercom and smart lock at the main door. Garden cameras cover the lawn and terrace; the courtyard camera covers the master-bedroom door. Indoor: a living-room camera and a baby monitor in the kids' room, both on a privacy schedule.

06 · Windows and shutters

Windows, garden doors and shutters

One system everywhere: thermally broken aluminium frames in dark bronze, to match the pergola, with double glazing and sliding insect screens. Every opening gets an aluminium roller shutter with foam-filled slats. Its box hides in the 50 cm lintel above the opening, so nothing shows inside, and the motorised ones have a manual override. The sun column gives the hours of direct sun on each opening on 21 June, 21 March and 21 December, with the five floors above taken into account. On the plans, a dashed line outside each opening marks its shutter. In the 3D model you can open, half-close or close them all.

08 · Before we commit

To confirm on site

  • Kitchen walls: structural engineer's sign-off to remove the two kitchen walls (3.76 m and 2.98 m).
  • Electricity board: can it move into the lobby cabinet, or does the cabinet wrap around it where it is?
  • Hood exhaust: duct route to the north façade through the lowered lobby and guest-WC ceilings.
  • Drainage: the dirty-kitchen sink, dishwasher and washer drain to the service shaft in the utility room.
  • Fibre entry: where the provider's cable enters, so the router bay sits on its path.
  • Garden: compound rules for the pergola, boundary screens and outdoor cameras.
